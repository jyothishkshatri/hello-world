A story of return

The path opened. The herd remembered.

After years of community mapping, native planting, and wildlife-safe road improvements, a historic migration route began to breathe again. Rangers watched three generations of elephants cross together—calmly, safely, and on their own terms.

“The happiest moment was not seeing the elephants arrive. It was seeing our children welcome them home.”
